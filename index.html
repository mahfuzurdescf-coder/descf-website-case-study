The challenge

One platform. Different reasons to visit.

Someone looking for a snake's name needs a different journey from someone exploring a conservation partnership. The information architecture makes these paths easy to discover without losing the connection between them.

Explore wildlife

Species profiles organise names, status, habitat information, and public safety guidance.

Understand programmes

Structured programme pages connect overview information with related articles and resources.

Learn and take part

Education, field stories, and collaboration pathways support visitors with different interests.

Design & engineering

Built around useful information.

A content-driven web platform designed for clarity, reusability, and responsible conservation communication.

01 / DISCOVERY

Searchable species guide

Bangla, local, English, and scientific names help visitors start with the language they know. Status filters narrow the published guide.

02 / CONTENT

Reusable programme pages

Next.js page templates use structured content from Sanity, with shared components for articles and resources.

03 / RESPONSIBILITY

Safety-aware guidance

Public information encourages safe distance from wildlife and avoids unnecessary exposure of sensitive species locations.
